For 156 years, Hong Kong was a British colony.

It began in 1842, after China lost the Opium War.

In 1898, Britain leased the New Territories for 99 years.

In 1984, they agreed Hong Kong would return to China.

The promise: one country, two systems.

At midnight on July 1st, 1997, the flag changed.

6.5 million people woke up under new rule.

Their way of life was guaranteed for 50 years.
